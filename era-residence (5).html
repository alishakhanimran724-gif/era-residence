The Concept

Era Residences is a boutique gated community of only 25 homes, designed around privacy, wellbeing and timeless Mediterranean living.

Expressed in the arrangement of terraces, shaded courtyards and the way light moves through the day — contemporary architecture with warmth, texture and calm at its centre.

New Golden Mile
Between Clifton & Karachi

One of the most sought-after stretches of the Arabian Sea Coast, where the mountains meet the sea. Golf, marinas, beach clubs and the old town are all within a short drive, yet the setting stays green, quiet and private.
